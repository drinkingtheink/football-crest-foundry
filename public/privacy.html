Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Crest Foundry ("the Service") is a free crest-design tool. This policy explains what data the Service handles.

Using the Service without an account

You can use the Service without signing in. In that case, your saved designs are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are never sent to us.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional and lets you save designs to the cloud and sync them across devices. Authentication and storage are handled by Supabase on our behalf. When you sign in we store:

  • Your email address, provided by your sign-in method (e.g. Google), used to identify your account.
  • The crest designs you choose to save, including their configuration and a preview thumbnail.

We use this information solely to provide the save-and-sync feature. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile information (name, email address, profile picture) to create and identify your account. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved crest from within the app at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us at the link below.

Cookies and local storage

The Service uses your browser's local storage to keep your work and (if signed in) your login session. It does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions or data-deletion requests: contact the maker via drinkingtheink.com.
